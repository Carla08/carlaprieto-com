My projects

Yoseph, and the work before it

Main project, 2026–present

Yoseph AI

Yoseph is an AI copilot for migrants navigating the U.S. immigration system. Most people in that position don't know what questions to ask, not because answers don't exist, but because the context is missing. A generic chatbot explains what a grace period is. Yoseph tells you how many days you have left and what to do today.

I co-founded Yoseph as a Delaware C-Corp and launched with 50+ users on day one, with zero paid acquisition. I'm the target user before I'm the founder: I built it while living my own transition from a TN visa to F-1.

Visit yoseph.ai
