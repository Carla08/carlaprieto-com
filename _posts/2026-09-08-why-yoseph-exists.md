In May 2026 I launched Yoseph with 50+ users on day one, with zero paid acquisition. That wasn't luck: it was having lived, in real time, the same question thousands of migrants ask themselves every day: "what do I do now?"

Most people navigating the U.S. immigration system don't know what questions to ask. Not because answers don't exist, but because context is missing. A generic chatbot explains what a grace period is. Yoseph tells you that you have 60 days and that day one starts today.

This blog is going to document the road: what works, what doesn't, and what I'm learning as a first-time founder.
